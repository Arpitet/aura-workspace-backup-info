Privacy policy

Aura Workspace Backup
Effective 23 October 2026.

Purpose and operator

Aura Workspace Backup is a personal desktop backup and restore tool operated by the owner of the authorized account. It is not offered as a public service.

Google data accessed

The tool uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. Within that scope, it creates, lists, reads and manages its authorized backup files and folders and obtains metadata needed for transfers, integrity checks and restores. It also uses account identity and storage-quota information to check the configured destination. It does not request unrestricted access to all Drive files.

How data is used

Data obtained through this OAuth client is used to back up, verify and restore the owner’s workspace and to maintain authorization for scheduled transfers. The client is used for this backup workflow, not model inference, advertising or profiling. The operator does not sell Google user data.

Storage and protection

Workspace contents are encrypted locally with restic before being uploaded to Google Drive. OAuth credentials and the repository password are stored separately in restricted local configuration; they are not published on this website. Operational records may include file paths, hashes, timestamps, errors and transfer statistics. Google processes the metadata needed to operate Drive under its own terms and privacy policy.

Disclosure

The owner’s encrypted backup repository is stored in the authorized Google Drive account. This website does not accept uploads, handle OAuth login or display backup contents. It includes no application analytics, advertising scripts or contact forms. A website hosting provider may retain ordinary web access logs under its own policy.

Retention and control

Backup versions are retained until the owner removes them under their chosen retention policy. The owner can stop scheduled transfers and revoke this application’s access through Google Account settings. Revoking OAuth access does not by itself delete existing cloud or local backups; the owner controls their separate deletion and any Drive trash retention.

Contact and changes

Contact the operator through the support email shown on the Google authorization screen. This page will be updated if the backup application’s data practices change.
